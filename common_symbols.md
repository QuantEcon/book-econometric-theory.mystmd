---
title: "Common Symbols"
label: c-cs
---

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 0

* - $P \implies Q$
  - $P$ implies $Q$
* - $P \iff Q$
  - $P \implies Q$ and $Q \implies P$
* - $a := 1$
  - $a$ is defined as equal to 1
* - $\RR$
  - all real numbers
* - $\NN$
  - the natural numbers $\{1, 2, \ldots \}$
* - $\range T$
  - range of function $T$
* - $\inner{\boldx, \boldy}$
  - inner product of $\boldx$ and $\boldy$
* - $\bolde_n$
  - $n$th canonical basis vector
* - $\|\boldx\|$
  - norm of $\boldx$
* - $\boldx \perp \boldy$
  - $\boldx$ and $\boldy$ are orthogonal
* - $S^{\perp}$
  - orthogonal complement of $S$
* - $\proj S$
  - orthogonal projection onto subspace $S$
* - $\boldI$
  - identity matrix
* - $\boldA^\T$
  - transpose of $\boldA$
* - $\diag(d_1, \ldots, d_N)$
  - diagonal matrix with diagonal elements $(d_1, \ldots, d_N)$
* - $\lL(x)$
  - distribution (or law) of random variable $x$
* - $\bB(\RR^N)$
  - Borel sets
* - $\bB$-measurable
  - Borel measurable
* - $\nN(\mu, \sigma^2)$
  - normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$
* - $\Phi$
  - standard normal CDF
* - $\phi$
  - standard normal density
* - $\1_B$
  - indicator function of set $B$
* - IID
  - independent and identically distributed
* - $\zdata$
  - data set
* - $\Zdata$
  - sample space
```
